PERSONAL WEBSITE REFLECTION

1. What did you learn from this activity?
I learned how to build a personal website using HTML and CSS. I also learned how to create navigation links, add images and videos, make a table, and organize my files.

2. What challenges did you face?
One challenge was getting the different sections of the website to look the way I wanted. I also had to make sure the links, images and video worked correctly.

3. How did you and your partner resolve challenges?
My partner and I discussed problems we had and shared ideas about the HTML and CSS. We helped each other when something was not working.

4. How did collaboration contribute to the project?
Working with a partner made it easier to compare ideas and notice things that could be improved. We could also help each other when we got stuck.

5. What would you improve if you had more time?
If I had more time, I would add more pages and more interactive features. I would also add more of my own projects and continue improving the design.
